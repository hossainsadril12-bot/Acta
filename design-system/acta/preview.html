Acta design system · v1

Quiet page. Glowing work.

A white, ruled page where the only thing that glows is what Acta produced. Violet light marks every summary, detected task, answer and draft, so "works in the background" becomes something you can see.

Principle 1

Violet means Acta did it.

The glow border and the sparkle appear on AI output only. Human messages are never violet.

Principle 2

The page is a ledger. The product is a card.

Page structure is square, white and ruled with hairlines. Product mocks are white cards with 20px corners and a soft stacked shadow.

Principle 3

Content is data.

Every string, list, price and link comes from content/site.json. Components accept any length and any count.

07 Rules

What keeps it Acta.

Do

  • Reserve the glow #B78AF7 and the sparkle for AI output only.
  • Keep page structure square and flat. All depth and roundness lives inside product cards.
  • Separate sections with full-bleed rail rules and 48px hatch bands, not color blocks.
  • Fill with #723AC5 only for conversion CTAs and the featured plan.
  • Render every string from content/site.json, with no copy in markup.

Don't

  • Add a second hue. No blue links and no green CTAs.
  • Use the accent as text on white. It fails contrast.
  • Set headlines above 600 or drop their negative tracking.
  • Ship touch targets under 44px or remove focus rings.
  • Hardcode counts like 3 bullets, 4 features or 3 plans. The content decides.
